Booyaka is designed to be easily customizable, allowing you to tailor its appearance and functionality to match your brand identity. This guide will walk you through the steps to white label Booyaka for your organization.

Booyaka is offered under a **dual license** model:

- **AGPLv3 License**: Free and open source. Requires copyright notices to remain intact and source code to be made available when distributing the software. [Read the terms of the AGPLv3 license](https://github.com/openpeeps/booyaka/blob/main/LICENSE)
- **Whitelabel Add-on**: Purchase a whitelabel add-on to the existing AGPLv3 license, allowing you to remove Booyaka branding while still complying with open source obligations.
- **Commercial License**: For organizations that wish to use Booyaka without the restrictions of the AGPLv3 license, allowing for proprietary modifications and distribution without disclosing source code.

If you wish to white label Booyaka and avoid AGPLv3 requirements, please [contact us](mailto:license@openpeeps.dev) for commercial licensing options.

## Whitelabel License

The **Booyaka Whitelabel License** is more like an add-on to the existing **AGPLv3 license**. This add-on is intended for organizations and teams that want to present Booyaka as their own product while still complying with open source obligations. **This license grants the following rights:**

- **Branding Removal:** Remove all Booyaka branding, logos, and copyright notices from your deployment.
- **Custom Branding:** Replace Booyaka’s visual identity with your own, including logos, colors, and product name, to create a seamless brand experience for your users.
- **Internal and Commercial Use:** Use Booyaka within your organization or as part of your commercial offerings, provided you comply with the AGPLv3 license terms.
- **Support:** Access to optional priority support and onboarding services (if applicable).

**Obligations:**
- You must continue to comply with the AGPLv3 license, including making source code available and retaining AGPLv3 notices in the source (even if not visible in the UI).
- You may not claim original authorship of Booyaka or misrepresent its origins.

**Restrictions:**
- You may not resell Booyaka as a standalone product.

## Commercial License
The Booyaka Commercial License is designed for organizations that require maximum flexibility and proprietary use of the software. This license grants the following rights:

- **Proprietary Use:** Use Booyaka in closed-source, commercial, or internal projects without the obligations of the AGPLv3 license.
- **No Source Code Disclosure:** You are not required to disclose your source code or modifications, even if you distribute Booyaka as part of your product or service.
- **Branding & White Labeling:** Remove all Booyaka branding and copyright notices, and fully rebrand the software as your own. You are obligated to not misrepresent the origin of the software. This includes replacing logos, colors, and product names.
- **Modification & Distribution:** Freely modify Booyaka and distribute it as part of your own products or services, including SaaS offerings.
- **No Copyleft Obligations:** You are not subject to the copyleft requirements of the AGPLv3 license.

**Restrictions:**
- You may not resell Booyaka as a standalone product.
- You may not claim original authorship of Booyaka or misrepresent its origins.
- You may not use the Booyaka name or logo in a way that implies endorsement or affiliation without permission.
